## 🛡️ INCUBATION INTERVIEW DEFENSE: THE 4 TOUGHEST QUESTIONS & KILLER ANSWERS

If the panel asks questions during the evaluation, deploy these exact answers:

### Q1: *"Why shouldn't students just use ChatGPT, Claude Code, or Multisim instead?"*
> **Answer:** *"ChatGPT and Claude are statistical language models that predict the next token; they cannot solve electrical circuit physics, calculate thermal junction runaway, or compute 2D matrix stiffness. When you ask ChatGPT about an op-amp, it gives you a generic textbook answer that often hallucinates pinouts.  
> Multisim, on the other hand, costs ₹6.5 Lakhs, is locked to 20 desktops in a physical lab room, and has a 30-minute drop-off curve for beginners.  
> PathPatch runs 100% in any browser in 3 seconds, evaluates real Modified Nodal Analysis physics, connects to physical hardware via WebSerial, and uses our MCP agent as a strict Socratic Flight Instructor that never leaks answers, preserving academic integrity."*

### Q2: *"How will you prevent students from burning real hardware in the college lab?"*
> **Answer:** *"We introduce 'Fly-By-Wire Breadboarding' using our sub-₹155 Smart Interposer Cartridge powered by a ₹13.80 WCH CH32V003 RISC-V coprocessor. It performs a 15-millisecond sub-threshold continuity check before mains power is applied. If a student reverses polarity or shorts Vcc to Ground, our cartridge electronically disconnects the rails in under 800 nanoseconds—faster than silicon fuse blow time—saving the IC and alerting the student in software."*

### Q3: *"How does our college pay for this when departmental software budgets are frozen?"*
> **Answer:** *"Colleges don't need to spend a single rupee from operational tuition fees. Under GFR 2017 Rule 130 and Ind AS 38, PathPatch is packaged as a 3-Year Capital Asset eligible for 100% funding under AICTE IDEA Lab Non-Recurring Equipment Grants or World Bank TEQIP-IV funds. Sourcing isolated departmental software costs ₹26 Lakhs; our unified campus license costs ₹4.2 Lakhs, saving the college ₹21.04 Lakhs in grant money while automating NBA SAR Criteria 3 and 6 accreditation evidence."*

### Q4: *"What is your realistic milestone during the 2-month post-exam incubation period (Dec & Jan)?"*
> **Answer:** *"In December, we will conduct a pilot trial across 120 students in VNRVJIET 2nd-year ECE labs during end-semester practical revisions, measuring reduction in component damage and student troubleshooting speed. In January, we will assemble 25 prototype Smart Interposer Cartridges in the college IoT lab and compile our first open-source synthesizable Verilog design for submission to the Tiny Tapeout SkyWater 130nm MPW foundry shuttle."*
